CONSUMER COMPLAINT UNDER CPA 2019
Complainant: Kunal Sengupta, Kolkata
Opposite Party: Nova Mobiles Ltd, Kolkata

FACTS:
Complainant bought smartphone for Rs. 40,000 on 10th January 2024 with bill attached.
Screen died after 1 month. Complainant alleges motherboard defect.
Opposite Party strongly disputes this, alleging liquid ingress and customer mishandling voided warranty.
No independent laboratory test report under Section 38 has been obtained.
Relief sought: Replacement phone.
