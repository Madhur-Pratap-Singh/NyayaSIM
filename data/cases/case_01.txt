CONSUMER COMPLAINT UNDER SECTION 35, CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019
BEFORE DISTRICT COMMISSION, NEW DELHI
Complainant: Rajesh Sharma, Preet Vihar, Delhi
Opposite Party: Apex Electronics Retail Ltd., Connaught Place, New Delhi

FACTS:
1. On 15th January 2024, Complainant purchased Apex Pro X Laptop for Rs. 85,000/- vide Tax Invoice No. APX/2024/0912 with a 2-year warranty card.
2. The laptop shuts down abruptly within 15 minutes of booting due to severe thermal throttling.
3. An authorized technician issued Service Job Sheet No. SRV-7712 confirming 'Manufacturing defect in heat pipe thermal mounting'.
4. Legal notice dated 20th February 2024 served via speed post demanding refund, delivered on 23rd February 2024.
5. Relief: Refund of Rs. 85,000/- with interest @ 12% p.a., compensation Rs. 30,000/-.

DOCUMENTS ATTACHED:
1. Tax Invoice No. APX/2024/0912 dated 15/01/2024.
2. Manufacturer 2-Year Warranty Certificate.
3. Service Job Sheet No. SRV-7712 confirming thermal defect.
4. Legal notice dated 20/02/2024 with postal delivery tracking receipt.
