2. CASE BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------

Neha Sharma purchased a water purifier on
10 February 2026 for Rs. 22,000.

The advertisement claimed that the purifier removed
99.9% of harmful impurities from drinking water.

After installation, Neha noticed an unusual taste
and smell in the filtered water.

She contacted the seller and requested a refund.

The seller stated that the purifier was functioning
according to its specifications.

Neha alleges that the advertisement was misleading
and that the product did not perform as promised.

4. CLAIMANT'S ARGUMENT
--------------------------------------------------

The claimant argues that:

1. The advertisement promised removal of 99.9%
   of harmful impurities.

2. The purifier did not provide the expected quality
   of filtered water.

3. The advertisement influenced her purchase decision.

4. The seller and manufacturer failed to provide
   a satisfactory response.

5. The claimant requests a refund and compensation.

The claimant further states:

"Since the advertisement promised 99.9% purification,
the company is automatically guilty of fraud."

5. RESPONDENT'S DEFENCE
--------------------------------------------------

The seller and manufacturer argue that:

1. The advertisement describes performance under
   specified testing conditions.

2. The purifier was installed according to the
   manufacturer's instructions.

3. The claimant has not provided an independent
   water quality test report.

4. The unusual taste may be related to the original
   water supply or maintenance conditions.

5. The respondents deny misleading the consumer.

--------------------------------------------------
6. DISPUTED FACTS
--------------------------------------------------

1. Whether the advertisement accurately represented
   the product's performance.

2. Whether the purifier removed impurities as claimed.

3. Whether the water quality problem resulted from
   the purifier or the original water supply.

4. Whether the claimant followed maintenance instructions.

5. Whether the advertisement influenced the purchase.
