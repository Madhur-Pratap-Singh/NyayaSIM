UNFAIR TRADE PRACTICE COMPLAINT UNDER SECTION 2(47), CPA 2019
Complainant: Tarun Mathur, Lucknow
Opposite Party: Deals24x7 E-Commerce Platform, Gurugram

FACTS:
1. Complainant booked a smartwatch on flash sale on 1st February 2024 for Rs. 4,999/- paid via UPI.
2. Opposite party failed to deliver the product, refused to issue a formal bill/tax invoice, and refused to process refund within the stipulated 7 days.
3. Email correspondence and bank transaction receipt attached.
4. Legal notice sent.
5. Relief: Refund of Rs. 4,999/- and Rs. 10,000 compensation for unfair trade practice under Section 2(47).
