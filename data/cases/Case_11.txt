FACTS OF THE CASE:

Simran Kaur purchased a refrigerator for Rs. 42,500 from CoolMart Electronics.

The refrigerator was delivered to her home two days later.

After removing the packaging, she noticed a dent on the left side and a crack near the vegetable compartment.

She immediately contacted the seller and requested replacement.

The claimant states that the delivery workers refused to record the damage.

The seller rejected the request, claiming that the damage may have occurred after delivery.

CoolMart states that the product was delivered in sealed packaging.

The seller claims that the claimant signed the delivery receipt without mentioning any damage.

The manufacturer requests a physical inspection before accepting responsibility.
