PRODUCT LIABILITY COMPLAINT UNDER SECTION 84, CPA 2019
Complainant: Meera Nair, Kochi
Opposite Party: FireVolt Accessories India Ltd, Bengaluru

FACTS:
1. Complainant purchased FireVolt Fast Charger on 12th January 2024 vide invoice attached.
2. While charging overnight, the charger burst into flames due to manufacturing capacitor defect, burning bedsheets and causing burn injuries to hand.
3. Hospital treatment bill and burns photo attached.
4. Formal notice issued.
5. Relief: Rs. 1,50,000 compensation under Section 84 for product liability injury and defect.
