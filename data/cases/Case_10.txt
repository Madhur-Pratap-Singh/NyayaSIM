CASE TITLE: Misleading Discount Advertisement for a Laptop

CASE TYPE: Consumer Dispute
CASE STATUS: Pending Review
CASE DATE: 18 March 2026

CLAIMANT: Karan Verma
RESPONDENT: TechZone Retail Store
MANUFACTURER: Nova Computing Pvt. Ltd.

FACTS OF THE CASE:

Karan Verma purchased a laptop for Rs. 65,000 after seeing an advertisement claiming a 40% discount.

The advertisement displayed an original price of Rs. 1,08,000.

After purchasing the laptop, Karan discovered that the same model was being sold by other retailers for approximately Rs. 68,000 without any special discount.

He believes that the advertised discount was misleading.

The claimant seeks compensation and an explanation regarding the advertised price.

RESPONDENT'S VERSION:

TechZone states that the advertised original price was based on its own pricing records.

The store claims that promotional prices may differ between retailers.

The manufacturer denies responsibility for the retailer's discount advertisement.
