Priya Khanna purchased an air conditioner for Rs. 38,000.

Within four days, the air conditioner started leaking water and failed to cool the room properly.

The claimant later demanded a complete refund, stating that the product was defective from the beginning.

The seller says that it offered another inspection, but the claimant refused to wait.
