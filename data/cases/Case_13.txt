FACTS OF THE CASE:

Nisha Sharma purchased a water purifier for Rs. 16,800.

The product advertisement claimed that the purifier removed 99.9% of harmful impurities.

After one month, the claimant noticed an unusual taste and smell in the filtered water.

She contacted the seller and requested a replacement.

The claimant alleges that the product failed to provide the advertised purification performance.

She also claims that the advertisement created an expectation of completely safe drinking water.

RESPONDENT'S VERSION:

The seller states that the product was installed according to the manufacturer's instructions.

The manufacturer says that water quality depends on the source water and maintenance conditions.

The manufacturer claims that the filters may require replacement.
