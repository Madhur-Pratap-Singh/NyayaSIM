FACTS OF THE CASE:

Dev Arora purchased a smartphone for Rs. 24,999.

After approximately two months, he noticed that the battery area had started swelling.

The phone's back panel became slightly separated from the body.

The claimant stopped using the device and contacted the seller.

He alleges that the battery was defective and that the product created a safety risk.

He requests a refund and compensation for the inconvenience.

RESPONDENT'S VERSION:

The seller states that the phone was sold in proper condition.

The manufacturer says that battery swelling may have multiple causes and requires technical examination.

The respondent also claims that the phone may have been exposed to excessive heat.
