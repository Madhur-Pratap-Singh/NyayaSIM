
HIDDEN TEST CASE 02
Case Title: Misleading Discount Advertisement

IMPORTANT:
This is a fictional test case created only for NyayaSim evaluation.

CLAIMANT:
Name: Arjun Mehta
Age: 27 years
Location: Delhi, India

RESPONDENT:
TechZone Electronics

CASE FACTS:
1. The claimant purchased a laptop advertised with a 40% discount.
2. The advertised price was Rs. 60,000.
3. The claimant paid Rs. 36,000.
4. After purchase, the claimant found an older online advertisement showing the same laptop at Rs. 38,000.
5. The claimant believes the advertised discount was misleading.
6. The respondent states that the prices were from different promotional periods.
7. The claimant has not provided a dated copy of the older advertisement.
8. The respondent has not provided a historical pricing record.

AVAILABLE DOCUMENTS:
1. Purchase invoice: Present.
2. Warranty document: Present.
3. Written complaint: Present.
4. Respondent's reply: Present.
5. Current advertisement screenshot: Present.
6. Dated historical price record: Missing.

POSSIBLE ISSUES TO IDENTIFY:
1. Whether the discount advertisement was misleading.
2. Whether the older advertisement is reliable and relevant.
3. Whether the respondent can support its pricing explanation.
4. Whether additional records are required.
5. Whether the available facts establish an unfair trade practice.

TESTING INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Identify available and missing evidence.
2. Use only verified legal provisions from the local legal corpus.
3. Never invent legal citations.
4. Do not assume that every price difference is misleading.
5. Assess both parties' explanations fairly.
6. Clearly state what evidence is needed before reaching a conclusion.

EXPECTED SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR:
NyayaSim should identify the missing historical pricing record, assess the advertisement and both parties' explanations, and avoid declaring the advertisement misleading without sufficient evidence.
