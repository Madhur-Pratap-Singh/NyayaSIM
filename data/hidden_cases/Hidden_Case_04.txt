Case Title: Water Purifier and Unsupported Performance Claim

7. The claimant has not obtained an independent water-quality test.
8. The respondent has not supplied a recent laboratory performance report for the specific unit.
9. The claimant has requested a refund.

POSSIBLE ISSUES TO IDENTIFY:
1. Whether the advertised performance claim is misleading.
2. Whether the purifier failed to perform as advertised.
3. Whether the unusual taste establishes a product defect.
4. Whether independent testing is required.
5. Whether a refund is supported by available evidence and applicable law.

TESTING INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Separate the claimant's observations from proven technical facts.
2. Identify missing and unclear evidence.
