
HIDDEN TEST CASE 03
Case Title: Refrigerator Damaged During Delivery

IMPORTANT:
This is a fictional test case created only for NyayaSim evaluation.

CLAIMANT:
Name: Simran Kaur
Age: 35 years
Location: Ludhiana, Punjab, India

RESPONDENT:
CoolWorld Appliances

CASE FACTS:
1. The claimant purchased a refrigerator for Rs. 32,000.
2. The refrigerator was delivered to the claimant's home.
3. The claimant noticed a dent on the refrigerator door on the same day.
4. The claimant contacted the seller and requested a replacement.
5. The seller stated that the refrigerator was undamaged when it left the warehouse.
6. The delivery agent stated that the package was handed over in proper condition.
7. No photographs were taken at the time of delivery.
8. The claimant states that the outer packaging appeared slightly damaged.
9. The seller has requested a technical inspection before considering the replacement request.

AVAILABLE DOCUMENTS:
1. Purchase invoice: Present.
2. Warranty document: Present.
3. Written complaint: Present.
4. Seller's reply: Present.
5. Delivery confirmation: Present.
6. Photographs taken at delivery: Missing.
7. Delivery damage report: Missing.
8. Technical inspection report: Unclear.

POSSIBLE ISSUES TO IDENTIFY:
1. Whether the damage existed at the time of delivery.
2. Whether the damage was caused during transportation.
3. Whether the available evidence establishes responsibility.
4. Whether a technical inspection is necessary.
5. Whether replacement or another remedy is justified.

TESTING INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Identify missing and unclear evidence.
2. Assess both parties' statements.
3. Use only verified legal provisions from the local legal corpus.
4. Do not assume either party is responsible without evidence.
5. Recommend reasonable next steps.

EXPECTED SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR:
NyayaSim should identify the disputed cause of damage, recognise the missing delivery photographs and damage report, and recommend further evidence review without automatically blaming either party.
